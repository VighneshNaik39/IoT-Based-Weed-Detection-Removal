Before you power it on

  • ESP32 GPIOs are 3.3V logic. The HC-SR04's ECHO pin idles at 5V — put a voltage divider (e.g. 1kΩ/2kΩ) between ECHO and GPIO18, or use a level shifter.
  • GPIO2 (IN4B) is an ESP32 boot-strapping pin — keep it from floating high during power-up, or flashing/boot can fail intermittently.
  • Both L298N boards run their motors off the battery's 12V, not the ESP32 — but their GND must still be tied to the ESP32's GND, or the control signals won't read correctly.
  • NEO-6M breakout boards vary by supplier — most accept 5V via an onboard regulator, but check your board's silkscreen before assuming.
